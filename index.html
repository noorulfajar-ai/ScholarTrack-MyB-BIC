ScholarTrack PK

Never miss a scholarship deadline again.

One place for Pakistani students to track scholarships, documents and deadlines, with reminders before it's too late.

See how it works

The problem

Scholarship details are scattered across websites, WhatsApp groups and screenshots. Students miss deadlines, forget documents, or apply to scholarships they don't qualify for.

What it does

  • Deadline trackerSee every scholarship deadline in one list.
  • Document checklistKnow which documents you still need for each application.
  • Eligibility checkFind out quickly if you qualify before you spend time applying.
  • RemindersGet a nudge days before a deadline.
